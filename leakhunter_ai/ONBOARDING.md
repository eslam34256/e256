# LeakHunter AI Pilot Onboarding

## 1. Inputs
Provide 3–6 months of:
- Supplier master
- Purchase orders
- Goods receipts
- Invoices
- Contracts / commercial terms

## 2. Pilot workflow
1. Load a controlled sample.
2. Reconcile PO ↔ Receipt ↔ Invoice.
3. Detect contract and supplier leakage.
4. Review evidence with Procurement/Finance.
5. Move findings through Potential → Validated → Realized.
6. Produce an Executive Savings Report.

## 3. Success metrics
- High-confidence leakage identified
- Findings validated by Procurement
- Realized savings/recovery
- False-positive rate
- Analyst review hours saved
- Supplier negotiations initiated

## 4. Important definition
Potential savings is an opportunity estimate. Realized savings must be validated by the customer and tied to an actual financial outcome.
